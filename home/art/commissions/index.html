🖼️ References

  • OCs must have a complete reference with proper colours. Without one it counts as a design commission, which costs more.
  • Please avoid pixel art or small sprites as the only references.
  • If you have a pose or composition in mind, please send images for that too.
  • Characters from media I can look up don't need a full ref sheet, but please let me know if you need any details altered.
  • No reference yet? You can come back to me once you have one.

📋 How It Works

  1. Inquire. Send the request form below. I'll reply within 3-5 days (the only exception is during exam periods, where I will be much less receptive). Your slot is only confirmed once I reply and approve it.
  2. Sketch. I'll send you a sketch. Revisions are welcome before this stage.
  3. Pay. After the sketch, I'll send a PayPal invoice (USD). Pay either 50/50 (first half after sketch, second half after the full render) or 100% after sketch. This is also applicable for Kofi.
  4. Colour & render. I confirm the sketch with you before I start colouring.
  5. Delivery. You get both a watermarked and an unwatermarked piece.

Do NOT send money before I approve your order and send an invoice. Unpaid invoices are cancelled after [48 hours].

🔁 Revisions

  • Before sketch: revisions are very welcome.
  • After sketch: small changes (colour tweaks, adding small details) are fine.
  • Major changes (full pose change, whole new colour palette) cost +10% each.
  • Please give feedback on each WIP! I can't fix details I wasn't told about.

⏰ Deadlines & Delivery

  • Turnaround is usually 1 month, 2 months at most. I'll tell you if there are delays.
  • Rush or deadline commissions depend on my availability. +20% if under 2 weeks.
  • No rush commissions in October, December, March, and May.
  • Please don't rush me! You can ask for updates, and I'll send them as often as I can.
  • Please save your files. I can't guarantee I'll keep them forever.

📜 Usage & Rights

You can
  • Use it for personal, non-commercial things: avatars, wallpapers, profiles.
  • Use it for video edits that you made yourself, with credit.
  • Print it for yourself, as long as nothing is sold.
  • Post it online with credit and a link to me. Please post the watermarked version.
  • Keep full rights to your character design.

You can't
  • Claim my art as yours, or remove my credit/signature.
  • Use it commercially in any way (selling, merch, monetised content, ads). Commercial licensing isn't offered right now, but will be added soon.
  • Use it for AI (training, validating, testing), NFTs, crypto, or anything blockchain-related. Do NOT feed my work to AI.
  • Trace it, use it as a base, or edit it. If you want a correction, just ask me.
  • Upload it to a gallery meant only for your own work.

My rights
  • I keep copyright. I may show the piece in my portfolio and use it as a sample, with credit to you for the character.
  • +60% for private commissions
  • Naming you publicly is your choice, so you may be labelled anonymously if you so choose for free.
  • I won't reuse or resell your character design.

⚠️ Other Terms

  • I can decline any commission that's out of my scope or makes me uncomfortable, and cancel anything that looks like a scam.
  • Refunds: none for completed art. If I cancel, you get a full refund.
  • No chargebacks. If there's a problem, contact me first.
  • Harassment or rude behaviour gets you blacklisted.
  • By commissioning me, you agree to all of the above.
